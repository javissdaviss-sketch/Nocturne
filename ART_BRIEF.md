# Nocturne asset and realism pass

The user's Agatha cyber-luxe boards establish crafted walnut and mahogany, aged brass, emerald upholstery, ivory ceramics, botanical interiors and restrained integrated electronics. Prioritize visual fidelity; laptop performance is not a design constraint for this pass.

Build separate, reusable 3D furniture and prop assets. Replace visible blockout furniture rather than dressing monolithic cubes with lines. Beds need a structural frame, shaped upholstery and draped bedding; cabinets need actual frame-and-panel construction, door reveals and hardware; tables need profiled edges, aprons and shaped legs. Small props need credible open interiors, wall thickness, seams, curved handles and continuous forms. Architectural boards and planar panels are valid construction elements.

Use a design, build, independent review and refinement cycle. Review assets in neutral three-quarter and close-up views, including clay views, then review every category in the world. Include service areas and gameplay objects outside the five established hero cameras. Preserve the original mystery, clue locations, physical approach paths, cable opening, cabinet/drawer behavior and saved progress.

The finished pass must be tested on actual Forward+ output, preserve graphics settings behavior, pass the gameplay and collision checks, and deploy through the existing release pipeline. Do not describe the result as photorealistic unless the rendered evidence supports that claim.
